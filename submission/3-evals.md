## 2. Backtest: the app vs today's system

12 weeks of synthetic demand (seed 7), replayed day by day. Both policies face exactly the same demand. The first 2 weeks are warm-up for the forecast and are excluded from every policy's numbers, leaving 70 item-days and 4134 plates of demand.

- **Today (cautious):** the mess cooks a fixed 40 paneer / 50 chicken, first come first served. Everyone is a walk-in.
- **Today (perfect average):** the mess cooks the true long-run average (53 / 63). It can't know this in real life; it's the best a fixed amount can do.
- **The app:** a forecast-driven purchase, pre-booking until 5 pm, cooking to bookings plus a walk-in buffer, and leftover raw stock carried over.

| Policy | Students turned away | Cooked plates wasted | Days short | Raw stock expired | Forecast error |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Today (cautious) | 934 (22.6%) | 0 | 69 of 70 | 0 kg | — |
| Today (perfect average) | 215 (5.2%) | 191 | 33 of 70 | 0 kg | — |
| The app | 91 (2.2%) | 56 | 3 of 70 | 0 kg | 12.5% |

**Reading it:** a cautious mess wastes nothing because it under-cooks, and 22.6% of demand is turned away. Cooking the average still turns away 215 and wastes 191 plates, because demand swings day to day. The app turns away 91 and wastes 56: bookings tell the kitchen the real number at 5 pm, and over-buying only leaves storable raw stock.

**Caveats:**
- The demand is synthetic, so these numbers show the mechanism, not a prediction for any real mess.
- The backtest assumes every booked student shows up. In the app, no-shows are charged anyway, so they cost the mess nothing.
- Unsold walk-in plates are counted as waste.

### Bugs the evals caught while building

1. **Censored demand.** History first recorded *confirmed* plates. On short days that hides real demand, so the forecast never learned it, and 143 of 1,869 seeded bookings got trimmed. It now records *requested* plates.
2. **Walk-in buffer stuck at zero.** The buffer was the average of walk-ins *sold*, which the buffer itself caps, so a zero buffer stayed zero forever: 439 students turned away in the backtest. It is now max(avg sold × 1.2, 10% of confirmed) and turns away 91.
